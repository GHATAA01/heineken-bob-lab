? What is IBM Bob?

Bob is an AI coding assistant that runs directly in VS Code — on your laptop, inside your workspace. It reads your files, understands your context, and helps you think, write, and build faster.

Think of Bob as a team member who has read every document in your project and is always available to answer questions, generate artefacts, or connect to external systems.

💡 Today you will experience Bob in three ways:
  • As an Analyst — ask questions, get structured answers from your files
  • As a Generator — one phrase produces a full discovery report or validation scorecard
  • As an Orchestrator — Bob calls a live external API through an MCP connection

0 Before You Start — Opening Bob IDE

✅ Bob IDE is already installed on your VM. You do not need to install anything. Your starter kit folder is also already on the VM desktop — ready to open.

Step 1 — Log in to your VM

Use the credentials your facilitator provided. Once the desktop loads, wait 30–60 seconds before opening anything.

⚠️ The VM takes a moment to fully load. Opening Bob IDE too quickly after login can cause it to start slowly. Give it 30–60 seconds first.

Step 2 — Open Bob IDE

Double-click the Bob IDE icon on the desktop. The IDE will open — it looks and works like VS Code, because it is built on it. You do not need to open VS Code separately.

💡 Bob's chat panel is on the left sidebar — it looks like a small chat bubble icon. Click it to open the chat. If you do not see it, press Ctrl + Shift + P, type Bob, and select Bob: Open Chat.

Step 3 — Open Your Starter Kit Folder

Your facilitator has placed a starter kit folder on the VM. Open it as your workspace:

  1. In Bob IDE, go to File → Open Folder
  2. Navigate to your starter kit folder — your facilitator will confirm the exact path on the VM
  3. Click Select Folder (Windows) or Open (macOS)

Once open, you will see the folder contents in the Explorer panel on the left. Bob will automatically read the .bob/ folder inside it — your skills and rules are loaded.

✅ You're ready when: Bob IDE is open, your starter kit folder is visible in the Explorer panel on the left, and you can see the Bob chat icon in the sidebar.
