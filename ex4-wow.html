▶ The Final Prompt

Open a new Bob chat. Copy and run the prompt below. It works across both team tracks — Bob will use whichever artefacts are present in your workspace.

Type this in the Bob chat:

Based on the BW discovery artefacts and the Foundation effort estimate we generated today, create a Sprint 1 story list for a two-week sprint.

Rules:
- Maximum 5 stories
- Each story must have: Story title, Team owner (BW or Databricks), Priority (High / Medium / Low), Estimated days
- Prioritise stories that unblock the most downstream work
- Format the output as a Markdown table

If both BW Discovery Outcome and Databricks Foundation artefacts are present, include at least one story from each team.

💡 Consider What Just Happened

Bob synthesised your artefacts — the discovery report, the complexity register, the effort estimate — into a prioritised sprint plan. Everything it used, you generated in the last 30 minutes.

⏱ Time to produce this manually: reading the discovery report, cross-referencing the effort estimate, mapping dependencies, and formatting for a planning session typically takes a senior BA the better part of a day. The output above is a first draft in under a minute — one that your team can review, challenge, and refine rather than starting from a blank page.
